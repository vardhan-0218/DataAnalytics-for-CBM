Controls to add:

1. Wear Rate
Type: Precision rotary knob with scale
Range: 0 → 0.02 A/unit
Purpose: Controls degradation speed
Label below control: Early vs Mid vs Late alert timing

2. Noise Level (σ)
Type: Rotary dial with tick marks
Range: 0.05 → 0.3 A
Purpose: Controls noise level for signal simulation
Label: False alarm vs stable detection

3. Operator Compensation
Type: Percentage rotary knob with digital display
Range: 0 → 30%
Purpose: Temporarily masks cycle time degradation
Label: System still detects degradation

4. Reset Frequency
Type: Industrial toggle or dial selector
Range: Low → High
Purpose: Temporarily hides late alerts
Label: NCR vs Normal operation

5. Design Strength
Type: Multi-position selector dial
Options:
Strong – 0.01 mm/1000h
Nominal – 0.03 mm/1000h
Weak – 0.06 mm/1000h
Purpose: Controls overall drift increase

6. Tolerance Sensitivity
Type: Three-position selector knob
Options:
Low (10%)
Medium (25%)
High (50%)
Purpose: Controls variation across machines
